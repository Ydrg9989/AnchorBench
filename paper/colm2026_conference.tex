\section*{Ethics statement}
This work studies a failure mode of LLMs that could lead to
systematically biased outputs in high-stakes applications.
Our benchmark uses synthetic scenarios in health, finance,
and operations domains; no real patient, financial, or
operational data is used.  We release the benchmark to enable
further research on LLM robustness.
