AALC at $\alpha{=}1.0$ brings \nai to near zero for all three

\label{tab:mitigation_nai}
\end{table}

Three key findings emerge:

\paragraph{Chain-of-thought amplifies anchoring.}
B3 (CoT) produces the highest \nai values across all models
(0.36--0.63), substantially exceeding baseline.  Explicit
step-by-step reasoning appears to give models more opportunity
to incorporate the anchor into their reasoning chain.

\paragraph{Prompt-level mitigations are inconsistent.}
B1 and B2 reduce anchoring for some models (Claude-3.5-Haiku,
Llama-3.1-8B via OpenRouter) but are ineffective or even
counterproductive for others (Llama-3.2-1B, Llama-3.2-3B).

\paragraph{AALC is the most effective mitigation for open-weight models.}
At $\alpha{=}1.0$, AALC reduces external-suite \nai from
0.14/0.35/0.28 to $-$0.02/0.01/0.01 for the 1B/3B/8B models,
representing near-complete debiasing.
At $\alpha{=}1.5$, mild over-correction is observed (negative \nai).

robust to position and format variations.
