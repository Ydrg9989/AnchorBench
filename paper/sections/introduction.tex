When asked to estimate a quantity, humans systematically shift their
answers toward arbitrary reference points---a phenomenon known as
\emph{anchoring bias}~\citep{tversky1974judgment, kahneman2011thinking}.
As LLMs are increasingly deployed in high-stakes domains such as
medical triage, financial risk assessment, and operational planning,
understanding whether they inherit this bias is both scientifically
interesting and practically important.

Recent work has begun to probe cognitive biases in
LLMs~\citep{jones2022capturing, hagendorff2023cognitive,
agrawal2024cognitive}, but existing studies typically (i)~test a single
anchor-delivery mechanism (usually prompt-embedded), (ii)~evaluate few
models, and (iii)~lack controlled ground-truth labels that separate
anchoring from mere accuracy degradation.

We address these gaps with the following contributions:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Controlled benchmark.}  A 108-item dataset
  (\textsc{AnchorBench-v0.2}) with five anchor-delivery suites
  (external, ICL, conversation-history, RAG, tool), three
  application fields (health, finance, operations), and
  gold-standard reference answers derived from latent signals.

\item \textbf{Multi-model evaluation.}  Systematic measurement of
  anchoring across six LLMs spanning three families and three scale
  tiers (1B--8B open-weight; GPT-4o-mini; Claude~3.5~Haiku),
  using the Normalized Anchoring Index (\nai), Total Variation
  Distance (\tvd), and Wasserstein-1 (\wone) metrics.

\item \textbf{Mitigation taxonomy and evaluation.}  Nine mitigation
  strategies organized into prompt-level (B1--B3), structural (EFR,
  CxDP), post-hoc (B4, PLD), and logit-level (AALC) categories,
  evaluated with bootstrap confidence intervals and
  Benjamini--Hochberg correction.

\item \textbf{Stress tests.}  Four robustness probes (anchor
  magnitude, position, format, RAG-snippet order) revealing
  model-specific vulnerabilities.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent This paper reports \emph{proof-of-concept} (PoC) results.
We plan to scale the benchmark to ${\geq}500$ items, add more
models, and extend to multi-turn RAG and tool-use settings.
