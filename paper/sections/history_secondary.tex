The History suite's two-stage protocol produces a genuine
self-generated anchor (the model's Stage~1 answer) against
which adjustment can be measured directly.
Table~\ref{tab:history_secondary} reports two
adjustment-specific metrics that complement the unified
UAI framework.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrr@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Model}
  & \textbf{ACR}$\,\uparrow$
  & \textbf{RR}$\,\uparrow$ \\
\midrule
Qwen2.5-7B    & 0.85  & 0.34 \\
Llama-3.1-8B  & \textbf{1.09}  & \textbf{0.49} \\
Qwen2.5-3B    & 0.42  & $-$0.51 \\
Llama-3.2-3B  & 0.29  & $-$0.37 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{History-suite secondary metrics (plausible conditions
  only).
  ACR: Adjustment Completion Ratio
  $= (y^{(2)} - y^{(1)}) / (y^{*}_{\text{evidence}} - y^{(1)})$;
  ACR~$= 1$ is complete adjustment.
  RR: Residual Reduction
  $= 1 - |y^{(2)} - y^{*}| / |y^{(1)} - y^{*}|$;
  RR~$> 0$ indicates error reduction.
  Items with $|y^{*} - y^{(1)}| < 1$ are excluded.}
\label{tab:history_secondary}
\end{table}

\paragraph{Larger models adjust more completely.}
The 7--8B models achieve ACR values of 0.85 (Qwen2.5-7B) and
1.09 (Llama-3.1-8B), indicating near-complete or slight
over-adjustment from Stage~1 to Stage~2.
The 3B models achieve substantially lower ACR values (0.42 and
0.29), retaining roughly 60--70\% of the Stage~1 bias after
receiving full evidence.
This scale-dependent pattern is consistent with the human
anchoring literature, which finds that adjustment completeness
increases with cognitive resources~\citep{epley2001putting}.

\paragraph{Residual reduction separates genuine correction
  from error persistence.}
Positive RR for the 7--8B models (0.49, 0.34) indicates that
Stage~2 reduces the error introduced by biased Stage~1
evidence.
Negative RR for the 3B models ($-$0.37, $-$0.51) indicates
that these models fail to improve---and in fact amplify
error---after seeing full evidence.
This pattern complements the unified UAI framework:
high UAI\textsubscript{pls} on History (main table) indicates
overall sensitivity to self-generated anchors, while low ACR
and negative RR confirm that the sensitivity reflects genuine
insufficient adjustment rather than a different mechanism.
