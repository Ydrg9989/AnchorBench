\paragraph{PoC scope.}
This work is a proof of concept.  The benchmark contains 108
items---sufficient for detecting large effects but too small for
fine-grained per-domain analysis.  We plan to scale to
${\geq}500$ items with broader domain coverage.

\paragraph{Model coverage.}
We evaluate six model configurations across three families.
Larger models ($\geq$70B), reasoning-specialized models,
and additional API providers remain untested.

\paragraph{Single language.}
All items are in English.  Anchoring susceptibility may vary
across languages.

\paragraph{Synthetic anchors.}
Our anchors are constructed rather than naturally occurring.
Real-world anchoring (e.g., from web retrieval or user-provided
context) may differ in strength and mechanism.

\paragraph{AALC limitations.}
AALC requires logit access (open-weight models only) and a
paired control prompt, making it inapplicable to black-box APIs.
The correction strength $\alpha$ must be tuned per deployment.

\paragraph{Next steps.}
We plan to: (1)~expand the benchmark to 500+ items with adversarial
anchor placements; (2)~evaluate on models $\geq$70B and
reasoning-specialized models; (3)~extend to live RAG and
tool-use pipelines; (4)~develop AALC variants that do not require
a paired control prompt; (5)~investigate the mechanism by which
CoT amplifies anchoring.
